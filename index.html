A Short Guide

The four families

Floral

Rose, jasmine, peony. Romantic and luminous — the language of bouquets, worn close.

Woody

Cedar, sandalwood, vetiver. Dry, grounded and calm — architecture for the skin.

Citrus

Lemon, yuzu, bergamot. Bright and weightless — morning light, bottled.

Oriental

Amber, oud, vanilla, spice. Warm and opulent — made for after dark.
